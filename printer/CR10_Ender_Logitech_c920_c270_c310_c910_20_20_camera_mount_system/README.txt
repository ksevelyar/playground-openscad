# Summary

After trying different ways to mount my Logitech c920 onto my Ender 3, I decided I wanted a way to mount the camera off the bed or Y axis. I came across @SCHEMENKE's design for an iPhone mount and thought it was a brilliant design. 

So, here is a remix of his tool-less mounting system with a way to attach the Logitech family of cameras you may already have lying around to the 20/20 rails on your Ender or CR-10. I have also included @Galbazig's c920 adapter in this remix to make things easy.

Print as many balls, spacers, and nuts as you want to customize your webcams reach and angle.

Belt tension mount can be found here:
https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3329210
